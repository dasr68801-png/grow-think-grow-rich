🕯️ Candlestick Basics

Each candle shows four key prices for a selected timeframe:

  • Open — where the period started
  • High — highest traded price
  • Low — lowest traded price
  • Close — where the period ended

Body: open-to-close range. Wick: price movement above or below the body.

📊 How a Stock Market Works

The basic market flow can be understood as:

1Company raises capital
2Shares are issued/listed
3Buyers & sellers place orders
4Exchange matches orders
5Price changes with supply & demand

Investors and traders can participate through brokers. Orders are routed to the relevant market infrastructure, where compatible buy and sell orders can be matched.

🧠 What Moves Stock Prices?

  • Company earnings and financial results
  • Business growth expectations
  • Economic and interest-rate conditions
  • Industry and sector developments
  • News, announcements and corporate actions
  • Market sentiment and buying/selling pressure
  • Liquidity and order flow

🕯️ Free Candlestick Patterns

Study the pattern first, then check the surrounding trend, support/resistance and volume or other context.

Hammer

Small body with a relatively long lower wick. Context matters; it can appear after a decline.

Inverted Hammer

Small body with a longer upper wick, commonly studied around a downtrend.

Shooting Star

Small body with a relatively long upper wick, typically studied after an advance.

Doji

Open and close are close together, showing a balance or hesitation during that period.

Bullish Engulfing

A bullish candle's body covers the prior bearish candle's body; study location and trend context.

Bearish Engulfing

A bearish candle's body covers the prior bullish candle's body; context is essential.

📈 Free Chart Patterns

Chart patterns are structures to study, not automatic buy/sell signals.

🔺 Triangle

Price compresses between converging boundaries. Study the breakout direction, volume and retest rather than assuming the outcome.

🟦 Rectangle

Price moves between relatively clear support and resistance. A break can be studied with confirmation and risk control.

👤 Head & Shoulders

A multi-swing structure often studied as a possible reversal setup. The neckline and market context are important.

🔻 Double Top

Two comparable highs around a resistance area. Confirmation is normally studied around the intervening support.

🔺 Double Bottom

Two comparable lows around a support area. Confirmation is normally studied after price moves above the intervening resistance.

📐 Wedge

Price forms converging trend boundaries. Direction, context and confirmation should be considered together.

🎯 Free Strategy Frameworks

These are educational frameworks for practice and backtesting—not guaranteed-profit strategies.

1️⃣ Trend + Pullback

  1. Identify the broader trend.
  2. Mark important support/resistance.
  3. Wait for a pullback.
  4. Look for price-action confirmation.
  5. Define stop-loss and position size before entry.

2️⃣ Breakout + Retest

  1. Mark a clear range or level.
  2. Wait for a genuine breakout.
  3. Study whether price retests the level.
  4. Look for confirmation.
  5. Plan risk before taking the trade.

3️⃣ Support/Resistance

  1. Identify significant price zones.
  2. Observe how price reacts there.
  3. Use candles/patterns only as confirmation.
  4. Avoid entering solely because price touched a level.
  5. Keep risk controlled and predefined.
